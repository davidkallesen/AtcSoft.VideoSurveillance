# AtcSoft.VideoSurveillance.Resources

The texts of the AtcSoft VideoSurveillance and CameraWall applications, in US English (neutral), British English (`en-GB`), Danish (`da-DK`) and German (`de-DE`).

Read them through the generated `AtcSoft.VideoSurveillance.Resources.Translations` class, or in WPF with Atc.Wpf: `{atcTranslation:Resx ResxName=AtcSoft.VideoSurveillance.Resources.Translations, Key=Cancel}`. A key's last digit is its number of parameters: `DeletedCamera1` takes `{0}`.
